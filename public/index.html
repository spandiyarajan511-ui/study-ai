AI Architecture

How Generative AI Works in StudyMate AI

Generative Artificial Intelligence leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) to synthesize new, customized learning materials from raw study notes. Here is the exact end-to-end process:

1

Student Input

Student pastes notes or uploads an academic PDF document.

2

PDF Extraction

Server extracts readable plain text from uploaded PDF binary data.

3

Prompt Engineering

Backend constructs a structured prompt enforcing strict educational constraints.

4

Gemini LLM Processing

Google Gemini analyzes semantic relationships in the student's text.

5

Content Generation

Gemini generates 5–6 summary points, 5 MCQs, and 3 key terms as structured JSON.

6

Interactive Dashboard

Frontend renders interactive quizzes, scoring, and copyable study summaries.
